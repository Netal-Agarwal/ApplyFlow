# Do-not-contact list. Copy to config/do_not_contact.txt (gitignored) and add one entry per line:
#   someone@company.com   never contact this address
#   @company.com          never contact anyone at this domain (or its subdomains)
# Blank lines and lines starting with # are ignored. Matching contacts are excluded from
# every contact list and draft, and the exclusion is noted in `explain-contact`.
#
# Examples (placeholders):
# jane.doe@example.com
# @example.org
